## Q1. French media ownership and editorial lines (Bolloré, CNews, Arcom, the Conseil d'État ruling, other owners)

### Takeaway
The Bolloré pole (CNews, Europe 1, JDD and others) is the clearest case of an owner moving outlets to the right. Regulators have answered with repeated fines and a landmark Conseil d'État ruling (13 Feb 2024) that widened the pluralism test to cover hosts, columnists and guests, not only politicians' speaking time. Critics on the left (Acrimed) describe a consolidated "pôle réactionnaire".

### Cited Findings
- **Conseil d'État ruling, 13 February 2024.** Acting on an appeal by Reporters sans frontières (RSF), the court ordered Arcom to re-examine within six months whether CNews meets its pluralism and independence obligations.
  - Pluralism must be judged across everyone on air (hosts, columnists, guests), not only the speaking time of political figures.
  - Independence must be judged on the channel's overall operating conditions and programming.
  - The court did not itself rule that CNews breached its obligations.
  - RSF had asked Arcom for a formal notice in 2021 and was refused in 2022.
  - Sources: [Conseil d'État (EN)](https://conseil-etat.fr/en/news/pluralism-and-independence-arcom-must-re-examine-compliance-with-its-obligations-by-cnews); [Conseil d'État (FR)](https://conseil-etat.fr/actualites/pluralisme-et-independance-de-l-information-l-arcom-devra-se-prononcer-a-nouveau-sur-le-respect-par-cnews-de-ses-obligations); [RSF](https://rsf.org/zh-hans/node/94154)
- **Arcom fines on CNews:**
  - 100,000 € for the programme "En quête d'esprit" (broadcast 25 Feb 2024) — [Arcom](https://www.arcom.fr/en/find-out-more/legal-area/decisions/en-quete-desprit-show-broadcast-february-25-2024-legal-sanctions-against-service-editor)
  - A separate 150,000 € sanction — [Le Club des Juristes](https://www.leclubdesjuristes.com/en-bref/cnews-sanctionne-par-larcom-a-hauteur-de-150-000-euros-7845/)
  - 10 Feb 2026: fines totalling 100,000 € over two "L'Heure des Pros 2 Été" segments (8 and 12 Aug 2025). Arcom judged them liable to incite hatred or discrimination against Algerians and Muslims. One outlet reports CNews's cumulative penalties at 630,001 € after these two fines — [Ozap](https://www.ozap.com/actu/des-propos-de-nature-a-inciter-a-la-haine-larcom-condamne-cnews-a-une-amende-de-100000-euros-apres-deux-emissions-de-lheure-des-pros-2/653646); [The Media Leader FR](https://fr.themedialeader.com/?p=210340)
  - A formal notice (mise en demeure) over its coverage of the Crépol case — [The Media Leader FR](https://fr.themedialeader.com/affaire-crepol-cnews-mise-en-demeure-par-larcom/)
- **JDD takeover, June-August 2023.** Lagardère, then being absorbed by Bolloré's Vivendi, named Geoffroy Lejeune, former editor of the far-right weekly Valeurs actuelles, to run the JDD. The newsroom struck for 40 days. AP called it the longest journalists' strike in France in decades. The strike ended in early August 2023 without success, and Lejeune stayed.
  - Sources: [AP via WSLS](https://www.wsls.com/business/2023/08/01/french-reporters-end-an-unsuccessful-strike-against-a-new-editor-known-as-a-far-right-supporter/); [La Libre](https://www.lalibre.be/international/europe/2023/06/25/en-france-la-redaction-du-journal-du-dimanche-jdd-refuse-de-virer-a-lextreme-droite-4KCRO7SZTVCH5AKBE5FIK6TZTM/); [Wikipedia: JDD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Journal_du_Dimanche)
- **Acrimed's view (2025).** Its magazine *Médiacritique(s)* no. 54 describes a consolidating "pôle réactionnaire" running "De CNews à (feu) la chaîne C8 en passant par le JDD et le JDNews, Europe 1, Sud Radio, Valeurs actuelles ou Frontières". Acrimed is a left-wing media-criticism group, so this is its framing — [Acrimed PDF](https://www.acrimed.org/IMG/pdf/mediacritiques54.pdf)

### Inferences
- For a Japanese reader, CNews and Europe 1 are not neutral wire-type outlets. They are opinion-heavy channels that a regulator has repeatedly sanctioned for content about Muslims and Algerians. Clips from them on riots should be read as editorial positions.
- The 2024 ruling matters because it moved French regulation from counting politicians' airtime toward judging a channel's overall editorial balance.

## Q2. Italian media: Mediaset, RAI/"TeleMeloni", newspapers

### Takeaway
The "TeleMeloni" charge rests on contested readings of Osservatorio di Pavia airtime data and on the April 2024 change to RAI par condicio rules. PagellaPolitica judged Meloni's defence ("partial and misleading"). Regulators AGCOM and the RAI oversight commission have moved toward qualitative pluralism criteria.

### Cited Findings
- **April 2024 par condicio change.** Ahead of the European elections, the RAI parliamentary oversight commission approved new par condicio rules by majority vote, with all opposition members voting against. The rules exempt current-affairs (approfondimento) programmes when they report on government and institutional activity — [L'Espresso](https://lespresso.it/c/politica/2024/4/10/il-governo-meloni-ha-cancellato-la-par-condicio-chi-comanda-potra-parlare-quanto-vuole/50630)
- **The "TeleMeloni" dispute.** Meloni cited Osservatorio di Pavia data to argue she appeared less on RAI news than her predecessors. The fact-checker PagellaPolitica called her claims "parziali e fuorvianti" (partial and misleading). La Notizia argues the Pavia data contradict her — [La Notizia](https://www.lanotiziagiornale.it/rai-giorgia-nega-lesistenza-di-telemeloni-ma-i-dati-dellosservatorio-di-pavia-la-smentiscono-su-tutta-la-linea)
- **Regulators' method.** AGCOM president Giacomo Lasorella's 2025 paper describes AGCOM and the oversight commission moving from purely quantitative airtime monitoring toward qualitative criteria after the 2024 European elections — [Nomos PDF](https://www.nomos-leattualitaneldiritto.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/LasorellaAnticipazioni2-2025.pdf)
- **Partisan readings of AGCOM data.** Libero, a right-leaning paper owned by Angelucci, uses AGCOM data to accuse La7 of favouring the "No" side in the justice referendum — [Libero](https://www.liberoquotidiano.it/news/politica/46852531/meloni-quarta-repubblica-pd-avs-senza-pudore-vergogna-senza-contraddittorio-pesante-sanzione/)
- **Trust in Italian news brands (Reuters Institute DNR 2025, published 17 June 2025).** ANSA is the most trusted brand for the eighth straight year (74%), followed by SkyTG24 and Il Sole 24 Ore (67% each) and TG La7 (61%) — [ANSA](https://www.ansa.it/amp/english/news/2025/06/17/ansa-remains-italys-most-trusted-news-brand-report_bca81cd8-73e7-464f-8a65-c1f33a3d4f05.html)

### Inferences
- Both sides use the same Pavia and AGCOM airtime data selectively. Japanese readers should treat "TeleMeloni" as a contested opposition label, not an established finding.

### Gaps
- I did not source these within budget:
  - Mediaset/MFE ownership by the Berlusconi family (Pier Silvio and Marina).
  - GEDI (Repubblica, La Stampa) under John Elkann/Exor, and the reported 2025 sale talks.
  - Corriere della Sera (Cairo/RCS).
  - Il Fatto Quotidiano's independent, M5S-leaning line.
  - The Angelucci ownership of Libero, Il Giornale and Il Tempo.
- I found no concrete content analysis of how Italian outlets covered protests against the security decree.
- I did not retrieve the RSF 2024-2026 press-freedom rankings for Italy.

## Q3. Foreign framing: Russian state media, US right and Musk, Japanese media and social media

### Takeaway
I found hard evidence of recycled or unrelated footage mislabelled as "France in civil war" during the 2023 Nahel riots (Indian fact-checkers documented it). Musk's well-known "civil war is inevitable" post was about the UK riots (Aug 2024), not France. RT France was shut in 2023, and Russian content moved to look-alike sites. I found no specific EUvsDisinfo case on riot coverage.

### Cited Findings
- **Musk's "civil war" post.** His "civil war is inevitable" post on X (4 Aug 2024) responded to UK riot footage. The UK government called it unjustified — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-08-05/uk-says-musk-s-civil-war-is-inevitable-post-is-unjustified). I found no fact-check of an equivalent Musk post about France.
- **Recycled footage during the 2023 French riots:**
  - A viral "France burning" building was the Manila central post office fire (May 2023) — [Factly](https://factly.in/unrelated-videos-are-shared-on-social-media-linking-them-to-the-ongoing-riots-in-france)
  - A "civil war in France" clip had already circulated in March 2023, and another clip was a parking-lot fire in Australia (Apr 2023) — [Newschecker](https://newschecker.in/fact-check/amid-riots-in-france-old-unrelated-videos-go-viral-on-social-media)
  - Newschecker verified about 10 riot-related false claims in six days, including film-shoot footage from 2016 — [Newschecker](https://newschecker.in/?p=16480386)
  - Old police-beating videos were presented as new — [The Quint](https://www.thequint.com/amp/story/news/webqoof/french-police-beating-protesting-after-week-of-rioting-fact-check); [BOOM](https://www.boomlive.in/tags/france-riots)
  - Old video was falsely tied to unrest after PSG's Champions League win (2025) — [Newschecker](https://newschecker.in/fact-check/old-video-falsely-linked-to-unrest-in-france-after-psgs-champions-league-win)
- **Russian state media in France:**
  - RT France was shut in April 2023 (bank accounts frozen under EU sanctions, then liquidated by a Nanterre court) — [EUvsDisinfo](https://euvsdisinfo.eu/report/rt-france-was-never-denounced-for-spreading-fake-news/); [NV](https://english.nv.ua/nation/russian-propaganda-channel-rt-frances-goes-bankrupt-50316540.html)
  - RT and Sputnik content was then copied onto new sites with no visible Moscow link — [Espreso](https://global.espreso.tv/omerta-and-reseau-international-emerge-as-new-pro-russian-media-in-kremlins-information-war)
  - In June 2023, France's Viginum attributed the "RRN/Doppelgänger" operation to Russia — [GMF ASD](https://securingdemocracy.gmfus.org/?p=23232); [SGDSN report](https://prod-site-internet-minarm-admin.cloud.defense.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/desinformation/Rapport%20Guerre%20en%20Ukraine_Trois%20ann%C3%A9es%20d%27op%C3%A9rations%20informationnelles%20russes%20-%20SGDSN.pdf)
- **Japanese-language commentary.** A Gendai column by Masuzoe Yoichi describes the 2023 riots in near-"civil war" terms and stresses that Nahel was second-generation Algerian — [Gendai](https://gendai.media/articles/-/112784). A JFIR commentary also exists — [JFIR](https://www.jfir.or.jp/cgi/m-bbs/index.php?no=5535)
- **Taiwanese coverage** noted that Macron blamed social media but did not address police conduct — [The News Lens](https://www.thenewslens.com/article/188345)

### Inferences
- The "France is in civil war / Europe is collapsing" image spread internationally is partly built from mislabelled footage. Sharers routinely recycle any fire or crowd video under "France" during unrest. This is the most concrete distortion to warn Japanese readers about.
- Search did not surface any direct "Musk on the France riots" evidence. The report should not attribute a France "civil war" post to Musk without a primary link.

### Gaps
- I found no specific RT/Sputnik riot articles or EUvsDisinfo cases framing the riots as "Europe collapsing".
- I did not systematically compare NHK, 産経 (Sankei) and 朝日 (Asahi) coverage, or analyse Japanese X (Twitter) narratives such as immigration-blame posts. Search returned only commentary columns. This needs a targeted search (e.g. 産経 フランス 暴動 移民 2023; NHK ナンテール).

## Q4. Typical distortions in domestic coverage

### Takeaway
Early French 24-hour news coverage of Nahel's death repeated the police prefecture's version (which the video contradicted) and a false claim about his criminal record. CNews commentators stressed the teenager's record. Elsewhere, unrelated footage inflated the scale of events.

### Cited Findings
- **Politis (June 2023).** Hours after the shooting, the first 24-hour news outlets relayed the prefecture's version without checking it. Some repeated a false claim about Nahel's record before retracting it. Politis also flags "chaos/émeute" as a framing choice — [Politis](https://www.politis.fr/articles/2023/06/mort-nahel-le-chaos-et-lemeute-les-tenants-dun-recit-mediatique-dangereux)
- **CNews.** Charlotte d'Ornellas said on CNews that Nahel had "un casier long comme le bras" (a criminal record as long as your arm). This is reported by TSA Algérie, a secondary source — [TSA](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/?p=240999)
- **The police account vs the video.** The police invoked self-defence, but a bystander's smartphone video contradicted that account and spread the protest — [Gendai](https://gendai.media/articles/-/112784)
- **Background.** Acrimed counted 161 invitations of police-union and security figures on BFM-TV, CNews, France Info and LCI between 1 Jan and 17 Mar 2019, during the Gilets jaunes period — [Acrimed Médiacritique(s) 31](https://www.acrimed.org/IMG/pdf/mediacritiques31.pdf)

### Gaps
- I found no systematic content analysis (Acrimed or academic) comparing Libération, Mediapart or L'Humanité with CNews and Le Figaro on the Nahel riots or the 2023 pension protests. I also found no documented examples of left media downplaying rioter violence.

## Q5. Public opinion data

### Takeaway
French opinion is nuanced. Majorities supported the Gilets jaunes early (about 70-75% approval in Nov 2018, falling to about 50% by 2019). About two-thirds opposed the 2023 pension reform. Majorities condemned the 2023 riot violence but stayed broadly favourable to the police, while also being angry about Nahel's death. "Bloquons tout" had a plurality of support (46%) in early Sept 2025. Distrust of media sits at about 61%. In Italy, about half reject the security decree and fear for the right to protest is widespread.

### Inferences
- The polls contradict the sensational "France vs its rioters" or "civil war" picture. Most French people condemned the riot violence and also felt anger at the police killing. Majorities backed the social movements (Gilets jaunes early on, the pension protests) while rejecting violence.
- Support for protests is strongly partisan and age-linked: high on the left, low among retirees ("Bloquons tout").
