# Actors and Political Interests Around Protests and Riots in France (and Italy), 2023-2026

Research note scope: the factions involved, their incentives, and the evidence for each. "VERIFIED" means an official or judicial finding, or consistent multi-source reporting. "ALLEGED" means the claim is one-sided, comes from a single source, or is unproven in court. I made 18 tool calls. Several primary sources (VIGINUM PDFs, Fondation Jean-Jaurès) could not be fetched; the Gaps sections say where.

## 1. Participants: unions, grassroots networks, ultra-left, ultra-right, farmers

### Takeaway
Unrest in France 2023-2026 involves distinct groups, each with its own aims:
- **Unions:** the CGT, Solidaires and FSU march, but cautiously, and they distrust leaderless movements.
- **Grassroots networks:** leaderless online movements such as "Bloquons tout" (10 Sept 2025).
- **Banlieue youth:** the Nahel riots (June-July 2023).
- **Ecological / ultra-left collectives:** for example Soulèvements de la Terre. The state tried to dissolve them and the courts overturned it.
- **Ultra-right groups:** they stage "expeditions" after violent crimes, such as Crépol in 2023.
- **Antifa groups:** the Jeune Garde case, Feb 2026.
- **Farm unions:** the FNSEA and the more militant, far-right-leaning Coordination rurale.

### Cited Findings
**Nahel riots (2023)**

**"Bloquons tout" (10 September 2025)**
- [VERIFIED] The main left parties (LFI, PS…) backed the initiative. The CGT, FSU and Solidaires joined the marches. CGT leader Sophie Binet voiced fear of far-right infiltration — [Universalis, "France: l'année politique 2025"](https://www.universalis.fr/encyclopedie/france-l-annee-politique-2025/6-tensions-sociales/)

**Soulèvements de la Terre (ecological / ultra-left)**
- [VERIFIED] Interior Minister Darmanin announced the dissolution on 21 June 2023. The Conseil d'État suspended it in référé (emergency procedure) on 11 Aug 2023 — [Conseil d'État](https://conseil-etat.fr/actualites/le-conseil-d-etat-suspend-en-refere-la-dissolution-des-soulevements-de-la-terre); [Reporterre](https://reporterre.net/Le-Conseil-d-Etat-suspend-en-refere-la-dissolution-des-Soulevements-de-la-Terre)

  - It accepted that the group's actions could legally count as provocation to violence against property.
  - It held the dissolution "disproportionate" to the "portée réelle" (actual reach) of those provocations.
  - It went against its own rapporteur public, and in the same hearing it upheld three other dissolutions.
  - Sources: [Reporterre](https://reporterre.net/La-dissolution-des-Soulevements-de-la-Terre-definitivement-annulee); [Actu-Juridique](https://www.actu-juridique.fr/libertes-publiques-ddh/flash-le-conseil-detat-annule-le-decret-de-dissolution-des-soulevements-de-la-terre/)

**Antifa vs. nationalists: the death of Quentin Deranque (Lyon, 14 Feb 2026)**
- He was a 23-year-old nationalist activist providing security for the Némésis collective at a protest against a Rima Hassan (LFI MEP) talk at Sciences Po Lyon. He died after an assault attributed to members of the Jeune Garde, an antifascist group founded by Raphaël Arnault, now an LFI deputy.

**Farmers (2024-2025)**
- In the Nov 2024 anti-Mercosur wave:
  - The FNSEA ran more than 80 actions, and Jeunes Agriculteurs backed the protests.
  - Coordination rurale (described as linked to the far right) used harder tactics: checking Spanish lorries and emptying wine, an A9 blockade at Le Boulou, and threats to ports, fuel depots and retail purchasing centres.
  - Coordination rurale chair Véronique Le Floc'h: "Our actions are getting tougher because it's a question of survival".
  - Sources: [The Local](https://www.thelocal.fr/20241120/french-farmers-call-for-more-protests-next-week); [The Local](https://www.thelocal.fr/20241118/french-farmers-restart-roadblocks-and-protests); [The Poultry Site](https://www.thepoultrysite.com/news/2024/11/french-farmers-continue-protests-over-eu-mercosur-trade-talks)
- A later wave, after most EU member states approved Mercosur over Paris's objection, saw tractors in Paris and port blockades (Le Havre, Bayonne, La Rochelle). The date could not be confirmed from the snippet — [Yahoo/AFP](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/hundreds-tractors-roll-paris-farmers-094328091.html)

**Italy**
- [VERIFIED] On 3 Oct 2025 the CGIL and USB called a general strike over the interception of the Gaza flotilla. The CGIL claimed over 2 million people in more than 100 demonstrations; police counted at least 80,000 in Rome against organisers' 300,000. Highways were blocked. The strike watchdog called the strike unlawful, and Landini said the CGIL would challenge it — [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2025/10/3/pressure-mounts-on-italian-govt-over-gaza-after-strike-paralyses-nation); [Al-Monitor](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2025/10/mass-protests-strikes-italy-gaza)

### Inferences
- Union strategy differs from that of horizontal movements. The CGT and others join leaderless mobilisations to avoid being outflanked, while warning of far-right capture. This echoes the Gilets jaunes dynamic.
- The state's reliance on dissolving groups (Soulèvements, Division Martel and others) gets mixed results in court. It works as a political signal more than as a lasting legal tool.
- Ultra-right "punitive expeditions" (Crépol) and antifa violence (Lyon 2026) feed a cycle in which each camp's violence becomes the other side's political ammunition.

## 2. Parties: framing and electoral gains

### Takeaway
After the 2023 riots, LFI refused to call for calm, which drew attacks from the government and from its PCF ally. Marine Le Pen came out of the riots with the highest approval of her attitude (41%, Ifop). After the 2026 Lyon killing, the RN, LR and parts of the moderate left demanded a "cordon sanitaire" against LFI. The right and the government consistently frame unrest as a law-and-order issue.

### Cited Findings
- Government minister Christophe Béchu denounced "Monsieur Mélenchon et ses tweets dans lesquels il refuse d'appeler au calme" (Mr Mélenchon and his tweets in which he refuses to call for calm) and called it "anti-républicain" — [Sud Radio](https://www.sudradio.fr/linvite-politique/emeutes-lfi-refuse-dappeler-au-calme-cest-anti-republicain-affirme-christophe-bechu)
- PCF leader Fabien Roussel publicly distanced himself from the insoumis who "ont refusé d'appeler au calme, certains allant jusqu'à dire que ces violences sont légitimes" (refused to call for calm, some going so far as to say this violence is legitimate). Mathilde Panot (LFI) retorted that he had no lessons to give — [Europe 1](https://www.europe1.fr/politique/fabien-roussel-se-desolidarise-des-propos-de-jean-luc-melenchon-sur-les-emeutes-4192422)
- Ifop-Fiducial for Sud Radio (July 2023): 41% were satisfied with Marine Le Pen's attitude during the riots, well ahead of Mélenchon. Le Pen emerged "la moins affaiblie" (the least weakened). This is a secondary summary, not the poll report itself — [Sud Radio](https://www.sudradio.fr/emission/soyez-libres-237)
- After the Lyon death (Feb 2026, three weeks before the municipal elections):
  - Bardella called for a "cordon sanitaire" against LFI.
  - Wauquiez (LR) asked all elected officials never to govern with LFI members.
  - Hollande and Glucksmann called for a break with LFI.
  - Bompard (LFI) said the affair "n'a absolument rien changé" (changed absolutely nothing) to its agenda.
  - LFI refused to exclude Arnault from its group.
  - Sources: [LCP](https://lcp.fr/actualites/mort-de-quentin-quelles-consequences-politiques-a-trois-semaines-des-municipales-431549); [Europe 1](https://www.europe1.fr/politique/mort-de-quentin-lassemblee-sembrase-lfi-mise-en-cause-898447)
- RN deputy Jean-Philippe Tanguy: Arnault "n'a jamais eu sa place à l'Assemblée" (never belonged in the Assembly) — [Sud Radio](https://www.sudradio.fr/linvite-politique/jean-philippe-tanguy-rn-raphael-arnault-na-jamais-eu-sa-place-a-lassemblee)
- A poll filed with the Commission des sondages asked whether LFI "entretient des liens trop étroits avec des mouvements qui recourent à la violence" (has overly close ties with movements that use violence). The notice gives no figures — [Commission des sondages](https://www.commission-des-sondages.fr/notices/medias/fichiers/add/2088)
- Mélenchon is increasingly rejected by a majority of French people in 2027 presidential polling (secondary aggregator) — [24matins](https://www.24matins.fr/sondages-presidentielle-2027-jean-luc-melenchon-de-plus-en-plus-rejete-par-une-majorite-de-francais-1366642)
- Historical parallel: Mélenchon's heavy presence around the Gilets jaunes "ne paie pas" (did not pay off) — [Public Sénat](https://www.publicsenat.fr/actualites/politique/l-omnipresence-de-melenchon-sur-les-gilets-jaunes-une-strategie-qui-ne-paie-pas)
- Italy: Meloni called the flotilla "dangerous, irresponsible" and mocked the strike, saying a long weekend and a revolution don't mix. Landini replied: "I never thought a prime minister would reach such a low level". Salvini warned strikers ahead of the general strike — [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2025/10/3/pressure-mounts-on-italian-govt-over-gaza-after-strike-paralyses-nation); [Wanted in Rome](https://www.wantedinrome.com/news/italys-salvini-issues-warning-ahead-of-general-strike-on-friday.html)

### Inferences
- The RN's strategy, consistent across 2023-2025, is to profit from security narratives while keeping distance from disorderly movements (it declined to join "Bloquons tout"). Its "respectability" posture yields better approval of its conduct than LFI gets.
- LFI mobilises social and banlieue anger. But its refusal to condemn violence, and the Lyon case, become a wedge that the RN, LR, the Macron camp and the social-democratic left use to isolate it. This is a cost in a second-round run-off logic.
- Retailleau (Interior Minister 2024-2025) used "law and order" framing to build a personal profile ahead of 2027.

- No Zemmour/Reconquête quotes were gathered.

## 3. Police unions and state security legislation

### Takeaway
On 30 June 2023, during the Nahel riots, the two main police unions issued a joint communiqué calling rioters "nuisibles" (pests/vermin) and "hordes sauvages" (savage hordes), declaring "nous sommes en guerre" (we are at war) and threatening "resistance". The left condemned it as seditious; the government did not react officially. In Italy, the Meloni government's security decree (DL 48/2025 → Law 80/2025) made road-blocking during protests a crime.

### Cited Findings
- [VERIFIED, wording via translation] The unions were Alliance (the second-largest police union by election results) and UNSA Police (the largest) — [Assemblée nationale written answer](https://questions.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/11/questions/QANR5L11QE63414.pdf)
- The communiqué's key lines (translated):
  - "Faced with these savage hordes, it's no longer enough to call for calm, it must be imposed".
  - "Today the police are in combat because we are at war. Tomorrow we will be in resistance".
  - A threat to act if the government did not take concrete measures to protect the officer charged.
  - Sources: [OpIndia (English translation)](https://www.opindia.com/2023/07/french-police-unions-official-statement-violent-protests-war-vermin/); [Military Watch](https://militarywatchmagazine.com/article/french-police-war-savage-hordes-vermin); [Ian Welsh](https://ianwelsh.net/tag/french-riots)
  - These sources are of uneven reliability; the original French text was not retrieved.
- Reactions:
  - The left called it "a threat of sedition", and the Greens' leader called it "a call to civil war".
  - Didier Fassin noted that no official reaction came from the government — [New Statesman](https://newstatesman.com/international-content/2023/07/france-forces-law-disorder)
  - The head of UNSA (the confederation) disavowed the statement — [Fox News](https://noticias.foxnews.com/world/enough-french-police-unions-condemn-savage-hordes-forcing-macron-action.print)
  - The UNSA Fonction publique also issued a 3 July 2023 statement — [UNSA-FP PDF](https://www.unsa-fp.org/resources/upload/20230703/64a2c76a6d57e.pdf)
- [VERIFIED] Italy: DL 48/2025 was converted into Law no. 80 of 9 June 2025.
  - Senate vote: 109-69-1.
  - Road-blocking by several people moved from an administrative offence to a crime, punishable by 6 months to 2 years.
  - Sources: [Diritto.it](https://www.diritto.it/approvato-decreto-sicurezza-emergenze-rischi-dubbi/); [WikiLabour analysis](https://www.wikilabour.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Garisto_Savoldelli_Decreto-Sicurezza.pdf)
- UN special rapporteurs sent a second letter urging repeal, saying the decree could disproportionately hit specific groups. This is a single secondary source — [L'Indipendente](https://www.lindipendente.online/2025/04/16/i-relatori-onu-di-nuovo-contro-il-dl-sicurezza-lede-i-diritti-umani-va-abrogato/)
- A hearing memo to the Camera said the provisions aim "to discourage civic and political engagement" — [Camera dei deputati](https://documenti.camera.it/leg19/documentiAcquisiti/COM01/Audizioni/leg19.com01.Audizioni.Memoria.PUBBLICO.ideGes.61833.23-04-2025-08-58-09.539.pdf)
- The Cassazione (Court of Cassation), in a non-binding report, said minor blockades that do not fully stop traffic are not punishable. A year on, constitutionality questions are pending — [SicurAUTO](https://www.sicurauto.it/news/codice-della-strada/decreto-sicurezza-cassazione-blocchi-stradali-non-punibili-se-lievi/); [La Notizia](https://www.lanotiziagiornale.it/?p=415021)
- For Italy's Oct 2025 strike, the Interior Ministry instructed prefects to prevent blockades — [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2025/10/3/pressure-mounts-on-italian-govt-over-gaza-after-strike-paralyses-nation)

### Inferences
- Police unions act as autonomous political players: they pressure the executive (on legal protection for officers) and use the moment of crisis to win concessions. The government's silence suggests it depends on police loyalty during unrest.
- Italy's Law 80/2025 directly criminalises a core tactic of the Oct 2025 strikes (road and highway blockades). This illustrates a "pass security laws in anticipation of unrest" pattern.

- No sources on post-2023 French legislative responses, such as parental-responsibility measures or the June 2024 proposals.

## 4. Foreign influence: verified vs. alleged

### Takeaway
French authorities have documented Russian-linked operations:
- the Stars of David stencils (Oct 2023), attributed to RRN/Doppelgänger amplification and a Moldovan-run network;
- the red hands on the Shoah Memorial (May 2024), for which Bulgarians were convicted in Oct 2025;
- the coffins at the Eiffel Tower (June 2024);
- the Storm-1516 and Matriochka disinformation networks.

VIGINUM attributed amplification of the May 2024 New Caledonia riots to the Azerbaijan-linked Baku Initiative Group. The documented pattern is amplification and opportunistic provocation, not causation of the riots.

### Cited Findings
**Stars of David (31 Oct 2023)**
- [VERIFIED attribution by the French Foreign Ministry] Blue Stars of David were stencilled in Paris and its suburbs on 31 Oct 2023. The Quai d'Orsay attributed their online amplification to the RRN (Recent Reliable News) network — [Europe 1](https://www.europe1.fr/node/2962)

### Inferences
- The verified pattern has three parts:
  1. low-cost physical provocations by paid proxies from Moldova, Bulgaria and possibly Serbia;
  2. amplification of these provocations through RRN/Doppelgänger networks;
  3. opportunistic amplification of existing unrest (New Caledonia).
- No verified evidence shows foreign actors caused or funded French riots or protests. Claims of "foreign funding" of protest movements remain unverified and should be treated as such.
- The real grievances behind the New Caledonia riots (the electoral-roll reform) predate the BIG campaign. Interference exploited the unrest; it did not create it.

## 5. Economic actors, and social media and state restrictions

### Takeaway
The 2023 riots were the costliest in French history for insurers: €793M and 16,400 claims, against €204M in 2005. Total damage is around €1bn (Senate; MEDEF also cited about €1bn). The damage fell mainly on businesses and local authorities. On social media, the state's main lasting move is a ban on social media for under-15s: adopted in July 2026, reported censured by the Conseil constitutionnel in Aug 2026, and re-notified to the EU in Sept 2026.

### Cited Findings
- [VERIFIED] France Assureurs' estimates rose over time:
  - €280M at first;
  - €650M (10 July 2023);
  - €730M with 15,600 claims (Sept 2023);
  - €793M with 16,400 claims (27 Mar 2024).
  - The 2005 riots cost €204M. The 2023 damage hit mainly commercial property and local authorities, not cars.
  - Sources: [Public Sénat on the Senate report](https://www.publicsenat.fr/actualites/parlementaire/emeutes-de-2023-un-cout-colossal-estime-a-un-milliard-deuros-selon-le-rapport-du-senat); [France Assureurs press release PDF](https://presse.tripalio.fr/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/20230906_france_assureurs_cp_violences_urbaines.pdf); [Atlas Mag](https://atlas-mag.net/fr/articles/emeutes-en-france-de-juin-2023-778-millions-usd-de-pertes-assurees)
- The Senate report puts total material cost at about €1bn, because not all damage was insured — [Public Sénat](https://www.publicsenat.fr/actualites/parlementaire/emeutes-de-2023-un-cout-colossal-estime-a-un-milliard-deuros-selon-le-rapport-du-senat)
- MEDEF estimated €1bn in damage — [Journal de l'économie](https://www.journaldeleconomie.fr/?p=29993)
- The under-15 social media ban:
  - The Assemblée nationale adopted the key article in Jan 2026. Vote counts differ between sources: 116-23 or 130-21 — [Hespress](https://fr.hespress.com/459961-france-lassemblee-nationale-adopte-linterdiction-des-reseaux-sociaux-aux-moins-de-15-ans.html); [AllAfrica](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202601280142.html)

### Inferences
- Insurers and retailers have a direct material interest in faster repression and in compensation schemes. MEDEF's €1bn figure supports pressure on the government for state aid and security.
- The under-15 ban is framed as child protection. It does, however, carry the post-2023 riot narrative that Snapchat and TikTok accelerated mobilisation and looting among minors. A direct causal link to the riots is not established in my sources.

### Gaps
